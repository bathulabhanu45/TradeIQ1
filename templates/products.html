Academic Integrity Notice: Profit Calculation Limitation

In alignment with strict database design standards, product procurement costs (Cost of Goods Sold / COGS) are not present in the transactional schema. TradeIQ strictly computes Net Sales, Gross Sales, and Unit Volumes rather than fabricating speculative profit margins. Profit margin analytics will be implemented in Phase 2 when vendor wholesale invoices are linked to catalog items.
