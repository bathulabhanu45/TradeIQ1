Accounting Governance & Exclusions Compliance

Verification of auditing standards implemented in SQL calculations.

1. Cancellation Exclusion Policy

Orders with status Cancelled are strictly excluded from gross sales, discounts, returns, and order counts as no commercial transfer occurred.

2. Returns Accounting Recognition

Returned orders remain in the valid_orders tally because physical fulfillment took place; returned monetary amounts are credited via total_returns.

3. Distinct Order De-Duplication

All order volume counts execute COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) to eliminate artificial inflation across parent-child multi-item joins.
